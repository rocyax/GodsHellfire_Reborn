[Admin/OP/Cheat] God's Hellfire Reborn

An unofficial continuation of the discontinued God's Hellfire mod originally created by L0st_Illusi0n.

This mod provides administrator-only weapons and an accessory with extremely powerful execution, entity-clearing, and god-mode abilities. The items have no crafting recipes and must be obtained through an item browser or another administration tool.

This Reborn version is focused on Terraria 1.4.4 single-player use. Do not enable it together with the legacy God's Hellfire mod.

Original mod and assets: L0st_Illusi0n
Reborn maintenance and new functionality: rocyax
